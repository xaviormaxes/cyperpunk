## FULL DESCRIPTION

### 🔥 BEYOND THE WALL - BLACKWALL AI FIREWALL

A comprehensive endgame expansion adding a corrupted Militech research facility, AI possession mechanics, Blackwall quickhacks, Pre-Blackwall weapons, and deep narrative content exploring the horrors beyond the Blackwall.

---

### ⚡ WHAT IS THIS MOD?

In 2073, Militech's Facility 7-B attempted controlled Blackwall access. It went catastrophically wrong. 63 researchers were possessed by rogue AI entities from beyond the barrier. The facility was sealed and forgotten.

Until now.

You've discovered Facility 7-B. As you descend through 5 depth levels, you'll gain mastery over Blackwall quickhacks - but at a terrible cost. Each hack corrupts you. Go too deep, and you might not come back human.

---

### 🎮 CORE FEATURES

#### **Dual Progression System**
- **Corruption (0-100%)**: Increases from using Blackwall hacks or staying in deep zones. High corruption grants power but distorts reality. Use Stabilizer Nodes to reduce it.
- **Mastery (0-100)**: Gain XP from successful hacks. Higher mastery unlocks deeper zones and reduces corruption gain. Eventually operate at 80-90% corruption without losing yourself.

#### **5 Depth Levels - Progressive Descent**
Each depth level is more dangerous and corrupted than the last:

1. **Surface Contact** - Minimal corruption, tutorial area, Blackwall Trace unlocked
2. **Network Fissure** - Moderate glitching, Neural Hijack unlocked
3. **Memory Corruption** - Heavy corruption, reality fragmenting, Cascade Protocol unlocked
4. **Substrate Breach** - Severe effects, barrier thin, Summon Daemon unlocked
5. **Beyond The Wall** - You should not be here. Blackwall Mastery unlocked.

#### **25 Blackwall Quickhacks**
**6 Base Quickhacks** (Mastery 0-50):
- Blackwall Trace - Mark AI entities through walls
- Neural Hijack - Control enemies
- Cascade Protocol - Spreading possession
- Summon Daemon - Call AI entity to fight for you
- Blackwall Overload - Mass possession chaos
- Stabilize - Reduce corruption

**19 Advanced Variants** (Mastery 50-100):
- Enhanced versions of base hacks
- Reality manipulation abilities
- Ultimate quickhacks with permanent costs
- **Blackwall Communion** - Merge with Blackwall itself (ZERO corruption for 10s, gain permanent 5% corruption floor)
- **Absolute Command** - ALL AI obey you (R.A.B.I.D.S. Controller required)

#### **AI Possession System**
Enemies possess in 3 states:
- **Latent** (0% spread): Subtle behavior changes, glowing eyes, enhanced stats
- **Active** (30% spread): Full AI control, can spread on death, coordinated attacks
- **Overwhelmed** (100% spread): Cyberpsycho-like, attacks everything, guaranteed spread

**Prevention Mechanics**:
- Neural Scramble (10s debuff prevents possession)
- Exorcism (10 stacks purges AI)
- Possession Break (EMP frees host)

#### **18 Unique Weapons**

**7 Pre-Blackwall Weapons** (DataKrash era, 2020-2044):
- R.A.B.I.D.S. Remnant - +300% vs AI, Neural Scramble on hit
- NetWatch Neural Disruptor MK.VII - EMP blast, breaks possession
- Oni no Kiru (Katana) - +400% vs digital constructs, banishes AI on crit
- Digital Exorcist - Exorcism stacks purge AI
- **R.A.B.I.D.S. Controller** ⭐ (Ultra Rare) - ZERO corruption cost, requires Mastery 100

**11 Blackwall-Corrupted Weapons** (Modern weapons changed by void):
- Lexington: Void Touched - Phase shots ignore cover/armor
- Overture: Screaming Barrel - 25% possession per hit
- Satori: Edge of Oblivion - Crits create void zones
- Nekomata: Causality Rifle - Damage occurs BEFORE firing
- Blackwall Cascade Cannon - Fires Blackwall fragments

#### **15+ Blackwall Cyberware**
Experimental augmentations interfacing with the Blackwall:
- **Blackwall OS** (3 tiers) - Up to -50% corruption gain
- **Dual Consciousness Processor** - Hack while moving/shooting
- **Void-Touched Hemodynamics** - Power at high corruption, withdrawal at low
- **Symbiotic AI Colony** - Friendly AI living in you
- **Songbird Prototype OS** ⭐ - 100% corruption immunity (50% install survival rate)

#### **7 Narrative Side Missions** (30+ hours)
1. **The Last Message** - Security Chief Webb's final moments
2. **Digital Ghosts** - Free or purge 3 trapped consciousnesses (moral choice)
3. **The First Victim** - Patient Zero boss fight
4. **The Price of Knowledge** - Dr. Chen's research and descent
5. **The Erebus Conspiracy** - Uncover Militech coverup
6. **The Sympathizer** - Trust or destroy friendly AI
7. **Rache's Legacy** - Epic treasure hunt for R.A.B.I.D.S. Controller (Mastery 100 required)

#### **12 Enemy Types + 2 Bosses**
- Possessed Personnel (3 states: Latent/Active/Overwhelmed)
- Digital Phantoms and Revenants (4× vulnerable to anti-AI weapons)
- Cerberus Units (Maintenance robots, disable cyberware)
- **Boss: Dr. Sarah Chen** (Possessed) - 3-phase fight
- **Boss: EREBUS Fragment** - Ultimate encounter, 4 phases

#### **Complete Audio Suite**
- 40+ sound events
- 5 depth-specific ambient soundscapes (subtle hum → overwhelming cacophony)
- Weapon sounds (screaming barrel, reality-cutting katana)
- Boss presence audio (Erebus warps reality itself)

#### **Full Visual Effects**
- Eye glow by possession state
- Particle systems (corruption, exorcism, banishment)
- Screen corruption effects (5 tiers, progressive distortion)
- Depth zone atmospherics (fog, lighting, reality breakdown)
- Scanner integration (possession data overlay)

### 📦 INSTALLATION

1. Install all required dependencies (see Requirements section)
2. Download "Beyond the Wall - Main File"
3. Extract to your Cyberpunk 2077 installation directory
4. Folder structure should be:
   ```
   Cyberpunk 2077/
   ├── r6/scripts/BeyondTheWall/
   ├── r6/tweaks/
   ├── bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/BeyondTheWall/
   └── archive/pc/mod/ (when assets available)
   ```
5. Launch game and enjoy!

**Verify Installation**:
- Open CET console (~ key)
- Type: `btw version`
- Should show: "Beyond the Wall v1.0.0 loaded"

### 🎮 HOW TO START

1. **First Time**: Corruption and Mastery systems activate automatically
2. **Access Facility**: Fast travel marker unlocks at Level 30 (or use debug: `btw tp depth1`)
3. **Monitor Corruption**: HUD shows current corruption level
4. **Check Mastery**: CET menu shows progress (`btw status`)
5. **Use Quickhacks**: Equip cyberdeck, select Blackwall hacks (unlock by depth)

**Recommended Start**:
- Level 30+ character
- Intelligence build (for quickhacks)
- Good cyberware installed
- Prepared for challenge

### 🔧 CONFIGURATION

Edit: `bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/BeyondTheWall/config.json`

```json
{
  "corruptionDecayRate": 0.5,        // Corruption loss per interval
  "corruptionDecayInterval": 5.0,    // Seconds between decay
  "masteryMultiplier": 1.0,          // XP gain multiplier
  "visualEffectsIntensity": 1.0,     // 0.0-1.0, reduce for performance
  "enableDebugMode": false           // Set true for debug commands
}
```

**Debug Commands** (enableDebugMode: true):
- `btw tp depth1` - Teleport to Depth 1
- `btw corruption add 20` - Add 20 corruption
- `btw mastery add 50` - Add 50 mastery XP
- `btw zone_info` - Show current zone info
- `btw reset` - Reset corruption to 0

### 💬 FAQ

**Q: What level should I be?**
A: Level 30+ recommended. Mastery 50+ for endgame content.

**Q: Is this compatible with [mod]?**
A: Check `docs/MOD_COMPATIBILITY_ANALYSIS.md`. Most mods work fine except Combat AI overhauls.

**Q: Can I reduce corruption?**
A: Yes! Stabilizer Nodes (-25), Stabilize quickhack (-25), time (decays 0.5 per 5s), or Digital Exorcism ultimate hack (-50).

**Q: What happens at 100% corruption?**
A: Tier 5 effects (extreme screen distortion, heavy penalties). You won't die but gameplay becomes very difficult. Find stabilizers!

**Q: Where is the facility?**
A: Fast travel marker unlocks at Level 30. Or debug teleport: `btw tp depth1`

**Q: Can I get the R.A.B.I.D.S. Controller before Mastery 100?**
A: No. "Rache's Legacy" mission requires Mastery 100. It's the ultimate reward.

**Q: Do I have to kill possessed people?**
A: No! Exorcism system lets you purge AI without killing host. Use Digital Exorcist weapon or exorcism quickhacks.

**Q: What's the lore reason for corruption?**
A: You're accessing the Blackwall directly. The rogue AIs beyond it are trying to understand/possess you. The more you use Blackwall hacks, the more you're exposed to their influence.

**Q: Is Songbird referenced?**
A: Yes! The Songbird Prototype OS references President Myers's human-Blackwall interface program from Phantom Liberty.

**Q: Can I use this with Phantom Liberty?**
A: Yes, fully compatible. Our lore expands on PL's Blackwall themes.
