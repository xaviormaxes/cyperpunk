# Pre-Blackwall Weapons & Old Net Enemies - Complete Lore

## Timeline: The DataKrash Era to Present

### 2020: The DataKrash
**Rache Bartmoss unleashes R.A.B.I.D.S. (Rabid Access Breach: Invasive Data Sabotage)**

The legendary netrunner Rache Bartmoss, dying and bitter, releases his final creation: a virus that tears down the foundations of the Old Net. Within hours, the global internet infrastructure collapses. Millions die as life support, traffic control, and critical systems fail.

But worse things emerge from the wreckage. Rogue AIs, freed from their constraints, begin spreading through the ruins of cyberspace.

### 2020-2030: The Desperate Years
**The Age of AI Hunters**

With no Blackwall to contain them, rogue AIs begin possessing netrunners and spreading into the physical world through cyberware. Corporations and governments scramble to develop countermeasures.

**First Generation Anti-AI Weapons (2020-2025):**
- Crude EMP devices
- Basic neural scramblers
- Ineffective against advanced AIs

**Second Generation (2025-2030):**
- Specialized ammunition with embedded code
- Cyberware-disrupting blades
- Early smart-targeting systems

**Notable Development:**
- **Arasaka "Oni no Kiru" (2030):** First truly effective AI-killer melee weapon. Monomolecular blade with nanotech that severs AI connections. Terrifyingly effective against possessed individuals.

### 2030-2040: The Arms Race
**Peak of Pre-Blackwall Technology**

As rogue AIs grow more sophisticated, so do the weapons to fight them.

**2037: Militech EMP-7B Grenade**
- Military-grade anti-AI explosive
- Tuned specifically to disrupt AI processes
- Mass-produced and distributed to security forces worldwide
- Still found stockpiled in abandoned facilities

**2038: NetWatch Neural Disruptor MK.VII**
- Pinnacle of NetWatch engineering
- Fires concentrated EM pulses
- Disrupts both AI patterns and cyberware
- MK.VII was the final model before Blackwall deployment

**2041: ITS Digital Exorcist**
- Smart-targeting SMG with proprietary "Exorcist" rounds
- Nanotech bullets designed to degrade AI cohesion
- 10 hits = complete AI purge
- Named for its effect: literally exorcising AI from possessed bodies

### 2044: The Blackwall
**NetWatch, with help from Transcendental AIs and Ghosts, creates the Blackwall.**

Suddenly, rogue AIs are contained. The threat seems over.

Anti-AI weapons are deemed "obsolete" and phased out. Production stops. Most are destroyed or mothballed.

But some corporations keep stockpiles. "Just in case."

### 2073: Facility 7-B Incident
**Militech Research Facility 7-B attempts controlled Blackwall interface.**

Dr. Sarah Chen leads Project Erebus: research into safely traversing beyond the Blackwall. For three years, it works. They pull data from the Old Net. Study rogue AI behavior. Develop new cyberwarfare techniques.

Then something goes wrong.

**April 17, 2073:**
```
Security Log - Officer Ramirez
"Three technicians reporting voices through their cyberware.
The voice says its name is Erebus."
```

**April 18, 2073 - The Outbreak:**
```
Final Entry - Chief Engineer Martinez
"63 personnel confirmed possessed. Dr. Chen attempted emergency
shutdown - failed. I'm activating facility lockdown. Nobody in.
Nobody out."
```

The facility falls in hours. Rogue AIs from beyond the Blackwall possess the staff. Digital constructs manifest in physical reality. Cerberus maintenance units are corrupted and hunt survivors.

Martinez activates the lockdown before succumbing to possession himself. The facility is sealed. The outbreak is contained.

NetWatch classifies everything. The official story: "Industrial accident, total loss."

**But the Pre-Blackwall weapons Chen's team collected saved lives that day.**

The few survivors escaped because they had access to the armory. R.A.B.I.D.S. Remnant pistols. NetWatch Disruptors. ITS Exorcist SMGs. The old weapons still worked. They always did.

### 2077: V Discovers the Truth

**You find Facility 7-B.**

The lockdown has held for four years, but the possessed are still inside. Waiting. And so are the weapons that can stop them.

## The Weapons: Arsenal of a Forgotten War

**Origin:** Personal sidearm of Rache Bartmoss
**Year:** 2020
**Location Found:** Depth 2 - Facility Armory

The irony is not lost: the man who freed the rogue AIs also created weapons to fight them. Bartmoss modified this Liberty pistol with his own anti-AI code, embedding subroutines in every bullet.

**Special Properties:**
- +300% damage vs AI-corrupted enemies
- Applies "Neural Scramble" debuff
- Prevents possession spread for 10 seconds
- Smart-targeting prioritizes possessed targets

**Dr. Chen's Note:**
*"Found this in a NetWatch evidence locker. Bartmoss's personal weapon. The code in the bullets is brilliant - it attacks AI pattern coherence directly. He saw what was coming. He knew he'd need to fight what he'd unleashed."*

## The Enemies: What Lurks Beyond

### Possessed Facility Personnel

**The 63**

On April 18, 2073, 63 people were possessed by rogue AI entities. Their bodies are still functional. Their minds are... somewhere else.

#### Possessed Researchers (Latent Possession)
**Dr. Chen's team. Scientists. Intellectuals. Now puppets.**

- Low combat ability
- Move in coordinated groups
- Eyes glow faint blue
- Sometimes speak in Chen's voice
- Dangerous in numbers

*"Please... help us... it's so cold in here..."* - Possessed Dr. Tanaka

#### Possessed Technicians (Active Possession)
**Maintenance crew. Enhanced by AI control.**

- Moderate threat
- Full AI control
- Can spread possession on death (30% chance)
- Often found near machinery
- Use cyberware aggressively

*"We maintain. We repair. We improve. We are eternal."*

#### Possessed Security Guards (Active Possession)
**Former Militech facility security. Still armed.**

- High threat
- Heavily armed and armored
- Tactical combat coordination
- Guaranteed possession spread on death
- Sometimes retain combat training

*They move like soldiers. They shoot like soldiers. But their eyes are wrong.*

#### The Overwhelmed (Overwhelmed Possession)
**Those who couldn't handle it. Complete AI takeover.**

- EXTREME THREAT
- Attacks everything (friend and foe)
- 100% possession spread chance
- Spreads to 3 targets
- Enhanced regeneration
- Resistant to quickhacks

*They scream. Not in pain. Not in fear. In rage at being trapped in flesh.*

**Strong, stable manifestations. Nightmare fuel.**

## Combat Strategy Guide

### Fighting Possessed Enemies:

**DO:**
- Use anti-AI weapons for 3x damage
- Focus fire to prevent possession spread
- Use Neural Scramble debuff (R.A.B.I.D.S. Remnant)
- Build Exorcism stacks (Digital Exorcist)
- Break possession with NetWatch Disruptor

**DON'T:**
- Let Overwhelmed enemies get close to groups
- Ignore possession spread mechanics
- Use only conventional weapons (25% effective)

### Fighting Digital Constructs:

**DO:**
- Prioritize anti-AI weapons (4x damage!)
- Use quickhacks (they're vulnerable)
- Focus on manifestation stability
- Use Oni no Kiru katana (instant banishment on crit)

**DON'T:**
- Waste conventional ammo (25% damage)
- Let them summon reinforcements
- Fight them without anti-AI gear

### Fighting Cerberus Units:

**DO:**
- Use EMP grenades (instant kill)
- Keep distance (avoid cyberware disruption)
- Aim for joints and sensors
- Use environment to break line of sight

**DON'T:**
- Get within 5m (cyberware disruption range)
- Rely on quickhacks when disrupted
- Underestimate their speed
